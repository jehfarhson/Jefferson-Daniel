How I Work

My Sales Approach

01

Targeted Prospecting

I build qualified outreach lists based on fit criteria, not volume. Every prospect I contact is one where I understand the use case and can articulate specific value from the first touchpoint.

02

Value-Led Discovery

I ask more questions than I give answers in early conversations. Understanding the prospect's actual problem — not assumed pain — is what separates closers from pitch-readers.

03

Structured Pipeline Management

Every opportunity lives in a defined stage with a clear next action and a realistic close date. This is how you close 20–35 clients in a month without losing track of a single conversation.

04

Retention as Revenue Strategy

Post-sale engagement is not an afterthought. I treat every closed client as the beginning of an account to grow — which is why I sustain 80–85% retention rather than constant re-acquisition.
